Commercial cleaning in San Antonio

San Antonio is where Chabod is based, and it covers a lot of ground: the Downtown and River Walk area, the Pearl district, the Medical Center, Stone Oak and the northwest side along Interstate 10 and Loop 410.

Spaces we clean here. Office suites, professional and medical offices (non-clinical areas), retail and showrooms, restaurants and food-prep kitchens, gyms and studios, churches and property-management common areas.

Access and scheduling. Downtown and Medical Center buildings often have front-desk security, parking rules or loading-dock hours, so we ask about them at the walkthrough and write the plan into the scope. Most suites clean best after hours, when your team has gone home.

Nearby. Downtown, the Pearl, Southtown, the Medical Center, Stone Oak and the Interstate 10 northwest corridor.

Commercial cleaning in Alamo Heights

Alamo Heights is a small independent city just north of Downtown, surrounded by San Antonio, with Olmos Park and Terrell Hills right next door.

Spaces we clean here. Boutiques, salons, restaurants, professional offices and small medical practices, plus churches and community buildings. These are mostly smaller, customer-facing spaces where floors, restrooms and entryways need to look right every morning.

Access and scheduling. Street-front shops usually need cleaning before opening or after closing, and many share walls or parking with neighbors. We confirm keys, alarm codes and quiet hours during the walkthrough so a visit never surprises a tenant.

Nearby. Olmos Park, Terrell Hills, the Broadway corridor and the neighborhoods just north of Downtown.

Commercial cleaning in Shavano Park

Shavano Park is a small city on the north side of San Antonio, in the Loop 1604 and Interstate 10 area.

Spaces we clean here. Professional and medical offices, small business suites and office-park buildings where a quiet, consistent clean matters more than a flashy one.

Access and scheduling. Office buildings in this part of town often use keycards, alarm systems and set building hours, so cleaning is normally scheduled in the evening or early morning. We document access and alarm procedures in the written scope before the first visit.

Nearby. The northwest side of San Antonio, Stone Oak, The Dominion area and the Interstate 10 corridor.

Commercial cleaning in Fair Oaks Ranch

Fair Oaks Ranch is a Hill Country community that spans Bexar, Comal and Kendall counties, along Interstate 10 northwest of San Antonio and a short drive from Boerne.

Spaces we clean here. Small offices, wellness and medical practices, studios, and community or association buildings. Many spaces here are compact, so the scope is usually short and detailed.

Access and scheduling. If your property is inside a gated or access-controlled area, we confirm gate procedures, codes and visitor rules at the walkthrough and put them in writing, so the same team can arrive without calling ahead each time.

Nearby. Boerne, the Interstate 10 corridor toward San Antonio and the Hill Country communities around it.

Commercial cleaning in Boerne

Boerne is the Kendall County seat, a Hill Country city northwest of San Antonio along Interstate 10, known for its historic Main Street.

Spaces we clean here. Main Street shops and restaurants, real estate and professional offices, medical and chiropractic offices (non-clinical areas), churches and event or community spaces.

Access and scheduling. Downtown storefronts are busiest on weekends and on Market Days, so a reset is often best on a slower weekday or early in the morning. We plan the day with you and write it into the scope, along with where keys and alarm codes are kept.

Nearby. Fair Oaks Ranch, Interstate 10 toward San Antonio and the surrounding Hill Country.

Commercial cleaning in Bulverde

Bulverde is a Comal County city along US 281, north of Loop 1604 on the way from San Antonio toward the Hill Country lakes.

Spaces we clean here. Small offices and retail along the highway, fitness, dance and gymnastics-style studios, medical and chiropractic offices (non-clinical areas), and church buildings.

Access and scheduling. Many businesses here are standalone buildings with their own entries and alarms rather than shared suites. We confirm key handling and alarm codes at the walkthrough and schedule around your opening hours and any evening classes or events.

Nearby. Spring Branch, Bracken Ridge and the US 281 corridor north of San Antonio.

Commercial cleaning in Spring Branch

Spring Branch is a Comal County community on the US 281 corridor, north of Bulverde.

Spaces we clean here. Hill Country storefronts, restaurants, small professional offices, churches and community halls: smaller spaces that benefit from a clear, short written scope and a predictable visit day.

Access and scheduling. Because it is a longer drive from the San Antonio side, we keep the visit day and the team consistent. Keys, alarm codes and after-hours entry are confirmed at the walkthrough and written into the scope.

Nearby. Bulverde, Bracken Ridge and the rest of Comal County along US 281.

Commercial cleaning in Bracken Ridge

Bracken Ridge is in Comal County, in the same area north and northeast of San Antonio as Bulverde and Spring Branch.

Spaces we clean here. Small offices, shops, studios and community or church buildings. If your space is not on this list of types, call and ask.

Access and scheduling. We handle each site individually: at a short walkthrough we confirm the address, the best day and hours, how we enter and where alarm codes and keys are kept, and we write that into the scope of work.

Nearby. Bulverde, Spring Branch and the surrounding Comal County communities.

Commercial cleaning in Schertz

Schertz is a fast-growing city northeast of San Antonio along Interstate 35, spread across Guadalupe, Bexar and Comal counties.

Spaces we clean here. Warehouse and light-industrial buildings with offices and restrooms, retail centers, medical and professional offices (non-clinical areas), fitness studios and churches.

Access and scheduling. Industrial and warehouse sites often have gates, shift changes and dock hours, so we schedule resets around the shifts and agree on which areas are cleaned while the building is quiet. Retail and office tenants are usually cleaned after closing.

Nearby. Cibolo, Converse, Universal City and the Interstate 35 corridor.

Commercial cleaning in Cibolo

Cibolo is in Guadalupe County, right next to Schertz, northeast of San Antonio.

Spaces we clean here. Retail strips, professional and medical offices (non-clinical areas), fitness and dance studios, restaurants and churches. Many newer buildings have open floor plans, glass storefronts and hard floors that show smudges quickly.

Access and scheduling. Newer suites often use keypads, smart locks or alarm apps instead of keys. We confirm exactly how access works for each tenant at the walkthrough and record who is authorized, so the written scope and the building agree.

Nearby. Schertz and the northeast side of the San Antonio area.

Commercial cleaning in Converse

Converse is in northeast Bexar County, in the Loop 1604 and Judson Road area, near Live Oak and Universal City.

Spaces we clean here. Neighborhood retail, restaurants, professional offices, medical and chiropractic offices (non-clinical areas), churches and fitness studios.

Access and scheduling. Strip-center tenants often share a landlord, a parking lot and sometimes a dumpster, so we ask who controls access and trash before the first visit. Most cleaning happens after closing, with access and alarm procedures confirmed in writing.

Nearby. Live Oak, Universal City, Schertz and the northeast side of San Antonio.

Commercial cleaning in La Vernia

La Vernia is a Wilson County town east of San Antonio on US 87.

Spaces we clean here. Main-street style shops and restaurants, small offices, churches and community halls. These are usually smaller buildings where a focused, well-defined scope works best.

Access and scheduling. Since La Vernia is a longer drive from San Antonio, we set a recurring day, keep the same team and confirm in advance how we enter, so visits are predictable for you and efficient for us. Keys and alarm codes are recorded in the scope.

Nearby. Saint Hedwig, Marion and the US 87 corridor east of San Antonio.

Commercial cleaning in Universal City

Spaces we clean here. Offices and shops serving local residents and the base community, medical and chiropractic offices (non-clinical areas), restaurants, gyms and churches.

Access and scheduling. Businesses here are mostly standalone or small-center spaces. We confirm hours, keys and alarm procedures at the walkthrough, and we write down any building rules about parking or deliveries so cleaning never interferes with your day.

Nearby. Live Oak, Converse, Schertz and the Randolph area.

Commercial cleaning in Live Oak

Live Oak is a small city in northeast Bexar County, bordered by Universal City and Converse.

Spaces we clean here. Shopping-center tenants, restaurants, small professional and medical offices (non-clinical areas), fitness studios and churches.

Access and scheduling. Shopping-center spaces usually need cleaning after closing, with entry through a back door or keypad. We confirm the entry method, the alarm and who to call if something looks wrong, and put it in the written scope.

Nearby. Universal City, Converse and the northeast side of San Antonio.

Commercial cleaning in Saint Hedwig

Saint Hedwig is a small Bexar County city about 18 miles east of San Antonio, near FM 1346.

Access and scheduling. Because it is outside the city, we plan the drive into the schedule and agree on a fixed visit day. We confirm keys, alarm codes and entry at the walkthrough so the team can work without delays.

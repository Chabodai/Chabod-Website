Chabod Cleaning Services LLC ("Chabod," "we," "us") is a family-owned cleaning company in San Antonio, Texas. This policy explains what information we collect on chabodcleaningservices.com and when you contact or book us, what we do with it, and what choices you have. We wrote it in plain English on purpose.

1. Who we are

Chabod Cleaning Services LLC, San Antonio, Texas. You can reach us at home@chabodcleaningservices.com or by phone or text at (210) 480-6224. For privacy requests, please email us.

2. What we collect

What you give us.

  • Your name, phone number, and email, when you fill in a form, call, text, or email us.
  • Details about the job, like the address of the property, its size and condition, and your access instructions (for example a lockbox or door code you choose to give us).
  • Billing records, like what we cleaned, the dates, and what was invoiced. We do not collect payment card numbers on this website.
  • The messages you send us by text, email, or phone.

What we create while we work. We take photos and video of our work, such as before-and-after photos and video walkthroughs. We use them to show the job was done, to check quality, and to record the condition of the property.

What our website tools collect. When the tools in section 4 are on, they collect technical information like your browser and device type, the pages you view, how you found us, which buttons you tap (call, text, menu, form), and your approximate location (city level). Except for GoHighLevel, they are not told your name, phone number, or email. GoHighLevel, described in section 4, also receives what you type into our forms, but only if you chose "Accept all."

If you email us about working with us, we use what you send to reply. This website has no job application form.

3. How we use it

  • To answer your questions and give you a quote.
  • To schedule and do the cleaning, and to contact you about it.
  • To invoice you and keep our records.
  • To check our work and to sort out a problem or a damage report, using our photos and records.
  • To understand how the website is used and to measure our ads, using the tools in section 4.
  • To connect your visit and your form to your contact record, so we can follow up with you, using GoHighLevel.
  • To follow the law, and to protect our business, our team, and our clients.

We do not sell your personal information for money. If you click "Accept all," we let Meta receive information about your visit so we can measure our ads. Some privacy laws call that "sharing" or a "sale." If you click "Reject non-essential," or your browser sends Global Privacy Control, that does not happen.

5. Cookies and similar tools

Cookies are small files a website saves in your browser. Other tools do the same job with browser storage. Some are needed to remember your choice. Others count visits or measure ads.

How your choice works.

  • Before you choose: only Google Analytics runs, to count visits, with Google's ad features turned off. Clarity and the Meta Pixel stay off. (Cloudflare's visit counter, described in section 4, uses no cookies.)
  • "Accept all": Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity, the Meta Pixel, and GoHighLevel can run.
  • "Reject non-essential": none of them run, and we delete the analytics cookies we can reach on our own domain.
  • Global Privacy Control: if your browser sends this signal, we treat it as "Reject non-essential." You do not need to click anything.

You can change your choice at any time with the "Cookie preferences" link at the bottom of every page: open Cookie preferences. Cookies that Meta, Microsoft, or Google put on their own domains can only be removed from your browser's settings.

Some browsers send a “Do Not Track” signal. There is no common standard for it, so our website does not change how it works when it sees one. We do honor Global Privacy Control as described above. When you accept all, the tools in section 4 that run after you accept may collect information about your activity over time and across websites.

6. Who we share it with

  • Providers that help us run the business, like the ones in section 4, only so they can do that job. This includes GoHighLevel, which keeps the contact records and messages we use to follow up with you.
  • Our team members, only the ones assigned to your job, who get the details they need to do it, such as the address and the access you gave us.
  • When the law requires it, or to protect our rights, our team, or others.
  • If the business is sold or merged, your information may go to the new owner.

We do not share your mobile phone number or your text-message opt-in or consent information with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. We share it only with service providers that help us run our forms and messages, and only for that purpose.

7. Text messages and emails

If you give us your phone number or text us, we may text you about your request, quotes, scheduling, and your service. If you also tick the optional box on our form, we may send occasional offers and updates. The full rules, including how to stop, are in our SMS Terms. We keep a record of when and how you agreed to texts.

Reply STOP to any text from us and we will stop texting you.

Emails. If you give us your email, we may email you about your request, quotes, scheduling, and reminders before your visit. If we send you offers or updates by email, every one of those emails will have an unsubscribe link, and you can also email us at home@chabodcleaningservices.com to stop them. We will stop within 10 business days, and we may still send you messages about a service you booked.

Our email and messaging tool, GoHighLevel, may record whether you opened an email we sent and whether you clicked a link in it. It does this with a small tracking image and with tracked links. We use it to see which reminders and messages reach people. Blocking images in your email app limits open tracking. This is separate from the website tools in section 4 and from your cookie choice.

8. Your privacy choices

  • Cookies and tracking tools: use "Cookie preferences" in the footer, or turn on Global Privacy Control in your browser.
  • Texts: reply STOP.
  • Access, correct, or delete your information: email home@chabodcleaningservices.com. We will answer within 45 days. We may ask a question or two to make sure the request really comes from you. Some records we may need to keep, for example to follow the law or to handle a dispute, and we will tell you if so.
  • Emails: use the unsubscribe link in any email, or email us.
  • Marketing: tell us you do not want offers and we will stop.

Depending on where you live, laws in Texas, California, and other states may give you rights to know what we hold, to correct or delete it, and to opt out of targeted advertising. You can use the choices above no matter where you live. We will not treat you worse for using them.

9. How long we keep it

We keep job photos and records for as long as we need them for the purposes above, generally at least 36 months.

We keep a record of when and how you agreed to texts. Other information stays with us only as long as we need it for the reasons in section 3. The tools in section 4 keep their data under their own settings.

10. Keeping it safe

We use reasonable steps to protect your information, and we only share job details with the team members assigned to the job. No website or system is completely safe, so please do not send us anything you would not want to share.

11. Children

This website is for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you think a child sent us something, email us and we will delete it.

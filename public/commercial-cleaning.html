Questions facility managers ask

What does commercial deep cleaning include?
Every site gets its own written scope, but a typical commercial deep clean covers restrooms (fixtures, partitions, grout and floors), break rooms and kitchenettes (appliances inside and out, sinks, cabinet fronts), hard and soft floors, interior glass and entry doors, and detail work such as baseboards, vents, switch plates and high-touch surfaces. The scope is agreed in writing after the walkthrough, so you know exactly what is and is not included.
Do you replace my janitorial company?
Not necessarily. Many of our clients keep a nightly or weekly janitorial service for trash, vacuuming and restroom restocking, and use Chabod for the recurring deep clean that daily crews are not scoped or scheduled to do. If you have no janitorial provider, we can discuss a broader recurring scope after the walkthrough.
What is a facility reset?
A facility reset is a scheduled deep clean that brings a space back to a known baseline: grime, buildup and detail areas that daily cleaning misses. Clients usually book it monthly or quarterly, or once before an inspection, an opening or a move-in.
How does pricing work?
We quote after we walk the space. Price depends on square footage, the scope of work, how often we clean, floor types, and when we can access the building. The proposal lists a clear price for the agreed scope, and we do not publish rates online because two spaces of the same size can need very different work.
How soon can you do a walkthrough?
Call or text Andrés and we schedule a walkthrough at a time that works for the facility manager or owner. The walkthrough is free and there is no obligation to move forward.
Who do I talk to after we start?
Andrés, directly. There is no account manager, call center or middleman. Your manager or facility contact can call or text him to report anything broken, ask for a scope change, or give feedback after any visit.
Can you clean after hours or on weekends?
Yes. Most commercial deep cleans are scheduled outside business hours so your team and customers are not disturbed. We confirm access, alarm codes and key handling during the walkthrough and put them in the written scope.
Are you insured?
We provide a certificate of insurance on request before the first visit, and can list your company as certificate holder. Tell us what your lease or vendor policy requires and we will confirm it in the proposal.
What chemicals do you use, and are safety data sheets available?
We tell you before the first visit which products we plan to use, and we can supply safety data sheets (SDS) for them. If your building has product restrictions, such as fragrance-free or approved-products-only policies, we work within them.
Do you require a contract or charge lock-in fees?
No contracts and no lock-in fees. We do sign the scope of work, which lists what gets cleaned, how often, the price and the schedule, so both sides know exactly what was agreed. If it stops working for you, you are not locked in.
Do you supply toilet paper, paper towels and soap, and your own equipment?
Either way works. We can provide restockables such as toilet paper, paper towels and hand soap, or you can provide them. The same goes for tools: we can bring our commercial vacuums and professional-grade chemicals, or use yours. It is written into the scope of work.
What areas of San Antonio do you serve?
We serve commercial spaces across San Antonio, including Alamo Heights, Shavano Park, Fair Oaks Ranch, Boerne, Bulverde, Spring Branch, Bracken Ridge, Schertz, Cibolo, Converse, La Vernia, Universal City, Live Oak, Saint Hedwig and Marion, and we have done recurring work for businesses as far north as Leander.
What kinds of businesses do you clean?
Offices, retail and showrooms, restaurants and food-prep kitchens, gymnastics and fitness studios, medical and chiropractic offices (non-clinical areas), print shops and sign studios, churches and community buildings, and property-management common areas. If your space is not on that list, call and ask.
What is not a fit for Chabod?
We do not do biohazard or crime-scene cleanup, clinical or medical-grade disinfection, construction debris removal, or exterior high-rise window work. We do clean the non-clinical areas of medical and chiropractic offices, such as reception, restrooms and pool areas. We say so up front instead of overpromising.

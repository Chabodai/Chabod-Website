1. About these terms

These Service Terms are between you and Chabod Cleaning Services LLC ("Chabod," "we," "us"), San Antonio, Texas. They apply to cleaning work we do for you: homes, short-term rentals (STRs), deep cleans, and move-in or move-out cleans. By booking us, or letting us start a visit, you agree to them. Our Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information, and our SMS Terms cover texts.

We call the people who do the cleaning "our team members."

2. Quotes and booking

A quote is based on what you tell us and show us about the property: its size, the rooms, its condition, and what you want done. We confirm a booking in writing, by text or email. If the property turns out to be very different from what we were told, we will tell you before we start and agree any change in price with you first.

3. The work and your part

We clean what is in the agreed scope: your quote, checklist, or written instructions. Anything outside that is not included, but we are happy to add it if you ask.

To help us do a good job, please:

  • make sure we can get to the areas to be cleaned;
  • secure pets;
  • put away cash, jewelry, and fragile or valuable items;
  • tell us about hazards or existing damage we should know about;
  • tell us about allergies, or scent and product sensitivities, so we can plan for them.

We may skip or stop an area that looks unsafe, and we will tell you why.

4. Access and keys

We do not receive or keep keys. Where a property uses a lockbox or a door code, we use the access you provide only for the scheduled visit, handle it under our internal procedures, and share it only with the team members assigned to the job. Please tell us if you change a code or lockbox. If we cannot get in, see section 6.

5. Payment

  • First visit. For any new client, payment for the first visit is due when that visit is completed.
  • Recurring service. If you have a standing weekly, biweekly, or monthly schedule, then after your first visit we invoice you at the start of each month for that month's scheduled visits. The invoice is due before the first scheduled visit of that month (prepaid).
  • One-time, deep-clean, and move-in/move-out. Payment is due when the work is completed.
  • STR hosts and property managers. We invoice each turnover when it is completed, and payment is due on completion.
  • How to pay. As shown on your invoice. We do not collect card numbers on this website.
  • Price changes. For recurring service, we give you 30 days' written notice (text or email counts) before the month a new price applies.
  • Overdue invoices. We do not charge a late fee. We may pause service until an overdue invoice is paid.
  • If we cancel or miss a prepaid visit, we will reschedule it within 7 days, or credit or refund that visit's price on your next invoice.

Deposits. New clients: we ask for a $50 booking deposit to hold the date. It is credited toward your first visit. If you cancel that booking, the deposit is not refunded. If we cancel or cannot do the work, we refund it in full. Recurring clients do not pay a deposit; their prepaid monthly invoices follow the rules in this section and in section 6.

6. Cancelling, rescheduling, pausing

We ask for 24 hours' notice if you need to cancel or move a visit. This is a courtesy request. There is no cancellation fee or lockout fee. A new client's $50 booking deposit is not refunded if that booking is cancelled (see section 5).

  • If you cancel a prepaid recurring visit with less than 24 hours' notice, we will reschedule it within 7 days if we can. If we cannot, we will credit that visit to your next invoice.
  • If we arrive and cannot get in, we will try to reach you and reschedule. If a client repeatedly leaves us without access, we may pause service.
  • You can pause or stop recurring service at any time with 24 hours' notice before the next visit. Amounts you prepaid for visits after that are credited or refunded within 14 days.
  • We may pause or end service if a property is unsafe, if our team is treated abusively, or after repeated unpaid invoices. If we end service, we credit or refund prepaid visits we do not perform.

7. If something is missed

Residential and one-time cleaning. If something in the agreed scope was missed or is not right, please tell us within 24 hours of the visit, and send photos if you can. That is a request, not a deadline. We want to hear from you any time, and the sooner you tell us, the easier it is to see what happened. We will come back within 48 hours at no charge to fix areas that were in the agreed scope.

STR hosts and property managers. Please tell us as soon as you find it, ideally within 24 hours, with photos. We return as soon as scheduling allows. When a guest is affected, we aim for the same day, subject to availability and access. We cannot guarantee timing.

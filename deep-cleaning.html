Frequently Asked Questions

What's actually included that isn't in a regular cleaning?
The level of scrubbing. Showers cleaned to a streak-free finish, baseboards throughout, corner-level dusting with zero cobwebs, full oven degreasing, outside cabinet degreasing, moving the fridge and stove when needed, and grout work in every bathroom. None of that is part of a standard recurring visit.
How long does a deep clean take?
Usually four to six hours with a team of two to four people. We adjust crew size and hours depending on urgency and how much notice we get.
Do I need to be home for it?
No. We just need a way in — a key left on-site or a temporary access code works fine.
Do you clean inside cabinets and the fridge?
If the property is vacant and cleared out, yes, fully, inside and out. If it's still occupied with belongings in place, we clean around your things but won't go inside personal food storage — purely a liability precaution. Clear the shelves and we'll go inside.
Do you provide documentation or proof for landlords?
Yes. Every deep clean includes a timestamped video walkthrough, timestamped photos, and a detailed invoice breaking down the work performed.
How far in advance do I need to book?
Sometimes we can accommodate same-day. Worst case, next-day. We understand move-outs come with hard deadlines, so we'll flex crew size and hours to make it work, as long as timing, access, and pricing line up.
Is this a one-time service, or do I have to commit to more?
Totally one-off, no contracts, no strings attached. Fair warning though — once you see the results, Andrés will absolutely try to charm you into becoming part of the Chabod family.

Questions people actually ask

What's the catch?
Honestly, there isn't one. This is a straightforward business that provides for my family and my team, and it stays that way because we do good work.
What does the name Chabod mean?
We're a faith-rooted, family-owned business — that's where the name comes from, and it's why we treat your property like it matters, not like a transaction.
Do you require a contract?
No. We don't lock clients into contracts or termination fees. Our clients stay because the work speaks for itself.
Can you handle same-day or last-minute turnovers?
Yes, always. We plug directly into your calendar through any PMS, so we see reservations the moment you do.
Will I get a different cleaner every time?
Usually not. Most properties get one dedicated team with a trained backup ready if needed, all working from the same written SOP.
How do you handle keys and property access?
We work around whatever system the owner already has — shared code, lockbox, smart lock, whatever it is. Every cleaner sent to a property is logged, so there's always a clear record of who was there and when.
What happens if a reservation comes in last-minute?
Covered by our two-system setup: manual calendar monitoring by a dedicated team member, plus Chabod's own automated system pulling the reservation and creating the cleaning task immediately.
Do you have a real process, or is this informal?
Every property gets its own written SOP, built personally by Andrés after meeting with the owner or property manager — covering everything down to bed-making and bathroom detail — so any trained team can execute it consistently.
